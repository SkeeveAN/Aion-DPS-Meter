skill_shapes.json - the floor shape (circle, cone, line, ...) of each monster skill.

Derived from dotnet/Assets/json/skill-shapes.json of the Waffle Aion 2 DPS meter
(https://github.com/Waffle-ens/waffle_meter), which read the zones from the game
client's SkillEffectFilter table. We kept one word per skill id.
